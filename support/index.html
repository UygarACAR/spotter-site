Support

Write to uygar0605@gmail.com. A person reads every message, usually within two working days. In the app, Profile → Settings → Support opens an email with your app version filled in.

How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Spotter, or in Spotter go to Profile → Settings → Spotter Premium → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

I paid but Premium isn't on.

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you paid with. If that doesn't work, email us and include the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report someone or something?

Use Report in the menu on any post, comment, profile, message, squad or challenge. We review reports within 24 hours and remove what breaks the rules. You can also block anyone: you will stop seeing each other everywhere in Spotter.

How do I delete my account?

In Spotter go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. It removes your account, workouts, posts, photos and everything stored on our servers. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account.

Does Spotter share my health data?

No. Data from Apple Health is only used inside Spotter for the features you turn on, and is never sold or used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email us and we will send it within 30 days.
