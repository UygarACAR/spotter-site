Workout tracker & log

Your squad is watching.

Spotter is a gym workout tracker for people who know what to do but don't always show up. Promise your week, train with your squad, and pay the forfeit if you miss.

Coming soon on the App Store for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Log every lift

Sets, reps, RPE, supersets, a rest timer that follows you to the Lock Screen, plates math and personal records.

Promise your week

Pick how many days you'll train. Your squad sees the streak, and a missed week gets called out.

Squads and forfeits

Three to six friends, one shared streak. Miss your week, buy the coffee.

Duels and challenges

Go head to head with a friend or join a challenge. Photo proof keeps everyone honest.

Smart progression

Know what to lift next session, based on what you actually did last time.

AI coach

Ask about your training and get answers built from your own log. Plans in minutes.
