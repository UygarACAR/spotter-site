Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

Spotter is a workout tracker with friends, squads and an AI coach. It is made by Uygar Acar ("we", "us"). You can reach us at uygar0605@icloud.com.

This policy says what Spotter collects, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. We wrote it to be read.

The short version

  • Spotter works without an account. Until you sign in, everything stays on your phone and nothing is sent to us.
  • If you sign in, we store your workouts and the things you choose to share, so they follow you across phones and your friends can see what you share.
  • We do not run ads, we do not track you across other apps and websites, and we do not sell your data.
  • Apple Health data stays on your phone. We never use it for ads and never sell it.
  • If you use the AI coach, a summary of your training and your chat are sent to Anthropic to write the answer.
  • You can delete your account, and everything on it, inside the app.

What stays on your phone

Spotter is local-first. Your workouts, routines, custom exercises, settings and progress are saved on your phone first. If you never sign in, they never leave it. Apple Health works on your phone only: Spotter can save your workouts to Health and read your body weight (to turn "2x bodyweight" into a number). Neither is uploaded. The photos you take for proof are kept on your phone until you share them.

What we collect when you sign in

You sign in with Apple. We do not have a password for you and never see one.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your advertising identifier, or what you do in other apps. Spotter has no analytics or advertising software in it. Our server's logs hold counts and error codes, never what anyone wrote and never keys or tokens.

How we use it

To run Spotter: sign you in, sync your data, show your friends what you chose to share, score challenges and squad weeks, send the notifications you asked for, answer you from the coach, and check your subscription. To keep it safe: review reports, remove content that breaks the rules, and close accounts that abuse it. To answer you when you write to us. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not build advertising profiles.

Who else sees it

Other people, as you choose. Your name, handle, bio, home gym, level and workout count are visible to people who are signed in. A post is visible to everyone, to your followers, or only to you, as you choose each time. Likes and comments are visible to people who can see the post. A nudge goes to one person. The people you follow, who follow you, or share a squad with can see how many training days you have done this week (a count, not the workouts). Squadmates see the squad's streak, callouts and forfeits. If you block someone, neither of you sees the other any more.

Our service providers. They handle data for us and may not use it for their own purposes.

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. Your data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Anthropic writes the AI coach's answers. If you use the coach, we send Anthropic: your first name, goal, experience level and units; this week's plan, progress and forfeit; your streak and workout count; a short summary of your last eight workouts (title, date, minutes, sets, volume, personal records) and up to ten lifts with estimated one-rep maxes; your routine names; squad names and the first names of squadmates who are behind or owe a forfeit; duel opponents' first names and scores; challenge titles and ranks; and your chat (up to twelve earlier messages and your question). For a program, your five answers and the exercise list. Anthropic sees an opaque code for your account, never your email or Apple ID. Anthropic keeps API data for a limited time under its own terms (Anthropic does not use this data to train its models, and keeps it for a limited period, currently up to 30 days, for safety and abuse monitoring).
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, the App Store (payments: we never see your card), Apple Push Notification service (it delivers notifications), and Apple Health (on your phone). Apple's own privacy policy applies to what Apple does.

The law and safety. We may share data when the law requires it, or to protect people from serious harm.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Reports, blocking and moderation

You can report any post, comment, person, message, squad or challenge. Reports are private: the person reported is not told who reported them. A person on our team reads reports, aims to act on them within 24 hours, removes content that breaks our rules and can close accounts. You can block anyone; they are not told. We keep reports so the people they protect stay protected. When an account is deleted, the reports it sent stay but no longer name it, and reports about it stay too. We delete a report 12 months after it is closed [SUGGESTED: 12 months, which is what the server does by default] after we close it.

Apple Health

Spotter asks permission before it uses Health. It can write your workouts to Health and read your body weight. This data stays on your phone. We do not upload it, we do not use it for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not give it to anyone. You can turn it off in Spotter (Profile, Settings, Apple Health) or in the Health app.

Push notifications

When you allow notifications and sign in, your phone's push token is stored with your account so our server can send you nudges, hype, squad alerts and challenge updates through Apple. You choose which kinds you want and quiet hours in Profile, Settings, Notifications. The token is removed when you sign out or delete your account, and Apple tells us when a token stops working. Delivery history is deleted after two weeks.

How long we keep it

  • Your account's data: until you delete the account. Deleting it is immediate and permanent: your profile, workouts, routines, posts, photos, comments, likes, follows, nudges, squads membership, challenge entries and settings are removed, and the Sign in with Apple link is revoked.
  • Backups: our provider keeps backups for up to 30 days; deleted data leaves them when they expire.
  • Reports: see above.
  • Notification history: two weeks.
  • Coach: we keep your Premium record and a count of daily requests as long as your account exists. We do not keep your chat on our server; it lives on your phone. Anthropic's retention is covered above.
  • Logs: counts and error codes only, for the period our provider keeps them.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Profile, Settings, Account, Delete account.
  • Edit your profile: Profile, Edit profile (name, handle, bio).
  • Choose who sees a post: on the Save workout screen, per workout.
  • Turn off notifications, Health, or the account's sync: in Spotter's Settings, or in iOS Settings.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction, or to object to or restrict how we use your data: write to uygar0605@icloud.com. We reply within 30 days.
  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland: you have the rights of access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction and objection under the GDPR, and the right to complain to your data protection authority. We process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), to keep it safe (legitimate interest), and where you allow it (consent, which you can withdraw).
  • If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law: you can ask what we hold, to correct or delete it. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

Spotter is not for children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Where your data goes

Our providers are in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) and in the United States (Anthropic, Apple). When data crosses a border we rely on the safeguards the law allows, such as standard contractual clauses.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Each person can read and change only their own private data: the database enforces it, not only the app. Our secret keys never ship in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies. The date at the top changes whenever the text does.
